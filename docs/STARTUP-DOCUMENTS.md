# BuyyMart — Documents Required to Start the Ecommerce Ecosystem

**Domain:** buyymart  
**Market assumed:** India (IST, GST, Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules, DPDP Act)  
**Purpose:** Every document you should have before you incorporate, open a bank account, connect payments, onboard sellers, and ship the first version of the app.

This is an operating checklist, not legal advice. Thresholds, tax rates, and filing rules change. Confirm the current position with a chartered accountant and a lawyer before you file or publish policies.

---

## 1. Decide the business shape first

Every later document depends on this choice. Write it down in one page before you register anything.

| Decision | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who sells the goods | Inventory (you buy and sell), marketplace (sellers sell, you facilitate), or hybrid | GST (including TCS), seller agreements, IT Act intermediary duties, liability for defects |
| Legal entity | Private Limited (recommended for a full ecosystem), LLP, or sole proprietorship | Investor readiness, director KYC, payment-gateway approval, liability |
| Channels | Website, Android, iOS, seller panel, admin, warehouse, delivery | App-store policies, privacy disclosures, role matrix |
| Categories at launch | General merchandise, grocery/food, electronics, fashion, pharmacy, jewellery | Extra licences (FSSAI, drug licence, BIS, hallmarking) |
| Geography | One state, all India, or cross-border | GST registration, import docs, customs |

**Recommended for a “complete ecosystem”:** Private Limited Company + hybrid model, with marketplace rules written even if you also hold your own inventory. Launch with a narrow category list so you do not collect licences you will not use.

## 2. Promoter identity documents

Collect these for every founder and proposed director before you apply for anything.

| Document | Why you need it |
| --- | --- |
| PAN card | Mandatory for DIN, company PAN, GST, bank, payment gateway |
| Aadhaar | KYC for DSC, bank, and most fintech onboarding |
| Passport (if available) | Foreign directors, and stronger address/identity proof |
| Recent passport-size photograph | DSC, MCA forms, bank |
| Address proof (Aadhaar, passport, utility bill, or bank statement, usually not older than 2–3 months) | Registered office and director KYC |
| Mobile number and email in the person’s own name | OTPs for MCA, GST, IT portal, and bank |
| Specimen signature | Bank and some DSC applications |

If a founder is an existing company or NRI, add: certificate of incorporation of that entity, board resolution to invest/subscribe, passport, overseas address proof, and FEMA/FDI notes from your CA before any share subscription from abroad.

## 3. Company formation documents

### 3.1 Before filing with MCA

| Document | What it must contain |
| --- | --- |
| Name application (SPICe+ / RUN) | Proposed names. “BuyyMart” should be checked on MCA and as a trademark before you pay for the company name. Include a short object clause: online retail, marketplace, logistics facilitation, payments facilitation as permitted by law. |
| Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for each director | Class 3, used to sign MCA and GST forms. |
| Director Identification Number (DIN) | Applied inside SPICe+ for new directors. |
| Registered office proof | Rent agreement or ownership deed **plus** a utility bill (electricity/gas/telephone) in the owner’s name, usually recent. NOC from the owner allowing use as registered office. |
| Memorandum of Association (MOA) | Objects of the company. Keep ecommerce, marketplace, warehousing, advertising, and related services inside the objects. |
| Articles of Association (AOA) | Share rights, director powers, transfer restrictions. Use a lawyer’s draft if you expect co-founders or investors. |
| Subscriber sheet and director consent | Who takes how many shares, and consent to act as director (DIR-2). |
| Declaration and affidavits | As required in the current SPICe+ form set (INC-9 and related declarations). |

### 3.2 Documents you receive after incorporation

Store PDFs and keep originals where the authority issues them.

| Document | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of Incorporation (COI) with CIN | Master identity of the company. Banks, GST, Play Store, Razorpay, and sellers will ask for it. |
| PAN of the company | Separate from founder PANs. |
| TAN | Needed once you deduct TDS (salaries, vendors, and section 194-O on marketplace sales). |
| MOA and AOA as registered | Attach to bank and investor packs. |
| First board resolution | Open bank account, appoint authorised signatory, adopt financial year, approve GST application. |
| Share certificates and register of members | Statutory registers under the Companies Act. Your CA/CS should set these up in the first month. |
| Statutory registers | Members, directors, charges, related-party contracts. |

### 3.3 Founder agreements (do these in the same week as incorporation)

| Document | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Founders’ / shareholders’ agreement | Equity split, vesting (typically 4 years, 1-year cliff), IP assignment to the company, non-compete/non-solicit as enforceable in India, leaver rules, decision rights. |
| IP assignment deed | Every founder, employee, and freelancer assigns code, designs, brand, and content to BuyyMart Private Limited. Without this, the company does not cleanly own the app. |
| Employment or consultant agreements | Role, salary or retainer, confidentiality, IP. |
| ESOP plan (can wait until first hire beyond founders) | Board and shareholder approvals before granting options. |

## 4. Tax, bank, and finance documents

| Document | When | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current account opening form + board resolution + COI + PAN + MOA/AOA + KYC of signatories | Immediately after COI | Operate only through the company account. Do not take customer money in a personal account. |
| GST registration (REG-01 and supporting proofs) | Before you invoice, and before sellers list if you are an ecommerce operator | Ecommerce operators and suppliers who sell through an ecommerce operator generally need GST even below the normal turnover threshold (CGST Act, section 24). Confirm with your CA. |
| GSTIN certificate | After approval | Print on invoices and show in the app footer and seller profile. |
| Letter of Undertaking (LUT) or bond | Only if you export services/goods without paying IGST | Not needed for a pure India retail launch. |
| E-invoice applicability check | When turnover crosses the notified limit | B2B invoices may need IRN/QR. Your CA will say when you cross it. |
| E-way bill enrolment | When you move goods above the threshold | Needed for your own inventory and for some seller shipments. |
| Accounting policy + chart of accounts | Before the first sale | Separate: product sales, shipping charged to customer, commission, payment-gateway fees, COD charges, GST output, TCS, TDS. |
| TCS setup under GST section 52 | If you are a marketplace (ecommerce operator) | You collect TCS on net taxable supplies of sellers and file GSTR-8. Your platform must store taxable value, GST, and TCS per order. |
| TDS under Income-tax section 194-O | If you are an ecommerce operator | Deduct TDS on gross sales facilitated, deposit it, and file returns. Rate and exemptions change — lock the rate in config only after CA confirmation. |
| Professional tax / shop establishment | State-specific, often within 30 days of starting the office | Example: Shops and Establishments Act registration in the state of the office. |
| Udyam (MSME) registration | Optional, soon after COI | Useful for some tenders, loans, and payment terms. Free on the Udyam portal. |
| DPIIT Startup India recognition | Optional | Needs incorporation, a short note on innovation/scalability, and sometimes a pitch deck. Helps with tax holiday eligibility review and credibility. Not a licence to sell. |

### Invoices and records you must be able to generate from the app

- Tax invoice (your own inventory sales) with supplier GSTIN, invoice number/date, place of supply, HSN, taxable value, CGST/SGST or IGST, total.
- Bill of supply or invoice as applicable for exempt goods.
- Commission invoice from BuyyMart to the seller (marketplace).
- Credit note for returns and cancellations.
- Receipt for payment collected.
- Seller settlement statement (gross, returns, commission, GST on commission, TCS, TDS, shipping, net payout).
- Credit note / refund trail that matches the payment gateway and the bank.

Retention: keep tax and company records for the period your CA specifies (commonly 8 years for tax). Do not design the database to hard-delete orders.

## 5. Local licences and category licences

Get these only for what you actually sell or operate. Extra licences you do not use still create filings.

| Licence / registration | Required if |
| --- | --- |
| Shops and Establishments registration | You have an office, warehouse, or staff in that state. |
| Trade licence (municipal) | Local body requires it for the office or warehouse. |
| GST registration in more states | You have a warehouse or hold inventory in that state (casual taxable person / additional place of business). Pure marketplace with no inventory usually registers where the company operates — confirm with CA. |
| FSSAI | You manufacture, store, distribute, or sell food (including packaged grocery). Central or state licence depends on turnover and whether you operate in more than one state. Sellers of food on your marketplace need their own FSSAI; you should collect and display the licence number. |
| Drug licence (state FDA / CDSCO rules) | Medicines, some medical devices. Do not enable this category until a specialist lawyer clears the model. |
| Legal Metrology registration | You pack or import packaged goods, or you are responsible for pre-packed commodity declarations (name, net quantity, MRP, customer care, month/year of manufacture or import). |
| BIS certification | Product categories under compulsory BIS (many electronics, helmets, and others). Collect the seller’s certificate; do not list uncertified compulsory items. |
| Hallmarking / BIS jewellery rules | Gold and silver jewellery. |
| WPC / ETA | Wireless products (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi devices) as applicable. |
| Plastic Waste Management registration | If you introduce plastic packaging into the market as a brand owner or importer. |
| Legal Metrology + country of origin | All imported goods: show country of origin to the customer before purchase (E-Commerce Rules). |

| Document | Action |
| --- | --- |

## 7. Customer-facing legal documents (must be live before payments)

Payment gateways reject websites that are missing these. Publish them at stable URLs, link them in the footer, and require acceptance at signup and checkout.

### 7.1 Terms of use (customers)

Must state:

- Legal name of the company, CIN, registered office, and whether BuyyMart is the seller, a marketplace, or both.
- Who the contract of sale is with (BuyyMart for inventory; the named seller for marketplace items).
- Eligibility (18+ for accounts; extra rules if you ever touch children’s data).
- Account registration, accurate information, one account per person unless you allow otherwise.
- Prices in INR, MRP versus selling price, what is included (tax, shipping, COD fee).
- When the contract is formed (usually when you confirm the order, not when the user taps Pay).
- Cancellation window before dispatch.
- Returns, refunds, and replacements: timelines, who pays return shipping, non-returnable categories (innerwear, personal care, perishables) consistent with Consumer Protection rules. You cannot contract out of statutory rights.
- Delivery estimates are estimates; title and risk transfer rules.
- Promo codes, cashback, and wallet: expiry, non-transfer, and that they are discounts, not deposits, unless you build a regulated wallet (see payments).
- Prohibited conduct: fake orders, coupon abuse, resale of stolen goods.
- Limitation of liability, as far as Indian law allows. Do not copy a US-only “we are never liable” clause.
- Governing law and courts (usually the city of the registered office) plus the consumer’s right to approach consumer commissions.
- How you change the terms (notice in app + date of last update on the page).

### 7.2 Privacy notice (DPDP Act 2023 and IT Act practice)

Write this as a notice, not only a policy buried in legalese. It should list:

- Data fiduciary: legal name and contact.
- Categories of personal data: name, phone, email, addresses, order history, device IDs, approximate location if you use it, support chats, KYC only if you collect it.
- Purposes: account, order fulfilment, delivery, fraud prevention, customer support, legal compliance, and marketing only with consent.
- Processors: payment gateway, SMS, email, cloud host, analytics, logistics partners. Name the categories even if vendor names change.
- Retention periods by category (account data, orders, logs).
- Rights: access, correction, erasure (subject to tax retention), grievance.
- How to withdraw consent for marketing.
- Children: if the service is not directed at children, say accounts are for adults and you do not knowingly collect children’s data.
- Cross-border: if servers or subprocessors are outside India, say so and on what basis.
- Grievance officer: name, email, and postal address. IT Rules expect an acknowledgement and a resolution timeline for intermediaries. Publish the same person on the site.
- Security practices in plain language (encryption in transit, access control). Do not claim “bank-grade” or “unhackable”.

### 7.3 Shipping and delivery policy

- Serviceable pin codes and how the app checks them.
- Dispatch time (for example, 24–48 hours) versus delivery time.
- Split shipments.
- Failed delivery and reattempt rules.
- COD: eligibility, limit per order, cancellation of COD if the buyer repeatedly refuses.
- What happens if the product is damaged in transit (photo within a defined window, refund or replacement).

### 7.4 Return, refund, and cancellation policy

- Cancellation before shipment: full refund to original method.
- Return window (many marketplaces use 7 days for fashion and longer for electronics defects; match the category).
- Refund timeline after the return is picked up or the warehouse accepts QC (state a number of business days, and also honour payment-aggregator timelines).
- Partial refunds, missing parts, and wrong item.
- Marketplace: who approves the return (seller vs BuyyMart) and that the customer still has a grievance path to you.

### 7.5 Grievance redressal policy

Required in substance by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and, if you are an intermediary marketplace, by the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

Publish:

- Grievance officer name, designation, email, phone, and address.
- Acknowledgement target (Rules for intermediaries use 24 hours for certain complaints and 15 days for resolution — follow the current text of the Rules).
- Escalation if the customer is not satisfied (consumer commission; and your internal nodal contact for government requests).
- A ticket ID on every complaint.

### 7.6 Other public pages payment gateways and app stores expect

| Page | Minimum content |
| --- | --- |
| About / Contact | Legal name, brand name, registered address, customer-care email and phone, hours. |
| Pricing and fees | Product price, delivery fee, COD fee, platform fee if any. No hidden mandatory charges. |
| Country of origin and seller details | On every product: seller legal name, address, GSTIN, and country of origin. |
| Cookie / tracking notice | If you use analytics or ads pixels, what they do and how to refuse non-essential cookies. |
| Community guidelines | Only if users post reviews or chat. You need a notice-and-takedown path. |

Product page fields the E-Commerce Rules expect you to show before purchase:

- Total price, with breakup of tax and other charges.
- Expiry or best-before for perishables.
- Country of origin.
- Seller identity and customer-care details.
- Return and refund terms specific enough to be meaningful.
- Accurate images and description; no fake reviews or rankings you have paid for without disclosure.

## 8. Marketplace documents (required if third parties sell on BuyyMart)

Skip this section only if BuyyMart is the sole seller of every SKU and you have no plan to onboard vendors.

### 8.1 Seller agreement (master terms)

Cover:

- Seller is the seller of record; BuyyMart is the platform and, where applicable, the limited collection agent for the sale price.
- Licence to list, and your right to delist, suspend, or withhold payout for fraud, counterfeit, or policy breach.
- Catalogue rules: genuine goods, authorised brands, no prohibited items (weapons, adult content if you ban it, medicines, wildlife, recalled goods).
- Pricing: MRP compliance, no predatory pricing clauses you cannot enforce, participation in campaigns is opt-in.
- Commission, shipping contribution, payment fee, and GST on commission. Attach a rate card as a schedule so you can revise it with notice.
- Settlement cycle (for example, T+7 after delivery, or after the return window). Hold amounts for open returns and claims.
- TCS and TDS: seller authorises deduction and receives statements.
- Returns and customer refunds: debit from seller settlement.
- IP warranty and indemnity if a brand owner sues.
- Data: seller gets only the customer data needed to fulfil that order, must not spam buyers, must delete it when fulfilment is done except where law requires retention.
- Insurance and product liability stay with the seller.
- Audit right on KYC and invoices.
- Term, termination, and payout of undisputed dues after termination.
- Dispute resolution between you and the seller (arbitration city, courts). Consumer disputes are separate and are not blocked by this clause.

### 8.2 Seller KYC pack (collect and verify before the first listing)

| Document | Check |
| --- | --- |
| PAN of the business | Name matches GST and bank |
| GSTIN certificate | Active status on the GST portal; legal name and address |
| COI / shop act / partnership deed / Udyam | Matches the entity type they claim |
| Cancelled cheque or bank letter | Account name matches the legal name |
| Address proof of principal place of business | Matches GST |
| Authorised signatory ID and a board resolution or authorisation letter | Person clicking “I agree” can bind the seller |
| FSSAI, drug licence, or BIS | Only for those categories, and not expired |
| Brand authorisation letter | For branded goods where counterfeit risk is high |

Store KYC in a restricted bucket. Do not show full PAN or account numbers to customers or other sellers.

### 8.3 Seller operating policies (shorter docs they must accept)

- Listing quality guide (titles, images, HSN, MRP, net quantity).
- Prohibited and restricted items list.
- Pricing and promotion rules.
- Shipping SLA by category.
- Penalty and strike matrix (late dispatch, cancellations, counterfeit).
- Payout and fee schedule.
- Catalogue content licence (you may resize images and show them in ads for the listing).

### 8.4 Brand-owner protection

- IP complaint form and a counter-notice process.
- Repeat-infringer suspension rule.

## 9. Payments, logistics, and vendor contracts

### 9.1 Payment gateway (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe, or similar)

They will ask for a packet. Prepare a single folder:

| Item | Typical requirement |
| --- | --- |
| COI, PAN, GST, MOA/AOA | Company proofs |
| Cancelled cheque and bank statement | Often last 3–6 months; new companies may use an account-opening letter |
| Director KYC | PAN, Aadhaar, photo |
| Website / app URL on HTTPS | Home, product, cart, contact |
| Privacy, terms, refund, shipping pages | Live, not PDF-only |
| Business description and expected monthly volume | Category, average order value, domestic only or not |
| Authorised signatory board resolution | Who signs the gateway agreement |

Product rules to write into your own payments spec:

- Do **not** store card numbers, CVV, or UPI PIN. Use the gateway’s checkout or tokenisation.
- PCI scope stays with the gateway. Your app stores order ID, payment ID, status, and last four digits only if the gateway returns them.
- Refunds go back to the original instrument.
- Webhook signing secret stored in a secret manager, not in the app binary.
- Reconciliation report: gateway settlement vs bank vs orders, daily.

**Wallets and gift cards:** a closed-loop promotional wallet that can only be used on BuyyMart and cannot be cashed out is a product design plus a clear policy. The moment users can withdraw, transfer to others, or hold a general balance, you are near prepaid-payment-instrument rules and need an RBI-authorised partner. Do not build a cash-out wallet in version 1.

**COD:** a written SOP for collection, remittance from the courier, and mismatch handling.

### 9.2 Logistics agreements

| Document | Parties |
| --- | --- |
| Master services agreement with the courier or aggregator (Shiprocket, Delhivery, Bluedart, and others) | Rates, slab weights, RTO charges, lost-shipment liability cap, COD remittance cycle, pickup SLA |
| Warehouse or 3PL agreement | If you store your own or sellers’ goods: bailment, insurance, shrinkage, inventory counts |
| Packaging SOP | Legal metrology declarations, invoice in the pouch, fragile handling |

### 9.3 Other vendor DPAs (data processing agreements)

Sign a short DPA or accept vendor terms consciously for:

- Cloud (hosting and object storage)
- SMS and email/WhatsApp
- Analytics and crash reporting
- Customer-support desk
- KYC / GST verification API

Each DPA should say what data is shared, why, retention, security, and whether data leaves India.

## 10. Data protection and security documents (build these before you write production code)

| Document | What “done” looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Data inventory (Record of processing) | Table: data element, purpose, lawful basis (consent / legitimate use under DPDP), where stored, who it is shared with, retention, owner. |
| Consent copy and logs | The exact sentence the user saw, version, timestamp, user ID. Needed to prove consent later. |
| Retention and deletion schedule | Orders and invoices: retain for tax. Marketing consent: until withdrawn. Support chats: define a period. Account deletion: delete or anonymise profile data that you are not required to keep, and confirm to the user. |
| Access-control matrix | Customer, seller, warehouse, support, admin, finance. Least privilege. No shared admin login. |
| Information security policy | Passwords, MFA for admin, encryption in transit, secrets, laptop rules, joiner/leaver access removal. |
| Incident response plan | Who is called, how you isolate, when you notify users and the board, and (when the DPDP Rules require it) the Data Protection Board. Include a 24-hour internal escalation. |
| Backup and restore note | What is backed up, how often, and a tested restore. |
| Vulnerability handling | How you take reports (`security@buyymart…`), and that you patch dependencies. |
| Employee confidentiality and acceptable-use policy | Signed before anyone gets production access. |

Do not collect Aadhaar of customers for ordinary shopping. Collect seller KYC only in the seller flow, with access limited to compliance staff.

## 11. Product and engineering documents required to start building

These are the documents that let a team build the same product without guessing.

### 11.1 One-page vision (write this first)

- Problem: why a buyer or seller uses BuyyMart instead of an existing marketplace.
- Who version 1 serves (city, category, language).
- What version 1 will not do (wallet cash-out, pharmacy, social feed, lending).
- Success measure for the first 90 days (for example, completed orders, not downloads).

### 11.2 Business requirements document (BRD)

Actors:

1. Customer
2. Seller
3. BuyyMart admin (catalogue, trust and safety, finance)
4. Warehouse or 3PL operator
5. Delivery partner (or the courier’s system)
6. Support agent

For each actor, list jobs they must complete. Example for the customer: search, filter, see seller and origin and total price, add to cart, pay or choose COD, track, return, raise a ticket.

### 11.3 Product requirements (PRD) by module

Write one short PRD section per module. Each section needs: user story, fields, states, and errors.

| Module | Must define |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Phone OTP login, email optional, session length, logout, account deletion request. |
| Addresses | Multiple addresses, pin-code serviceability, default address. |
| Catalogue | Category tree, product, variant (size/colour), SKU, MRP, selling price, HSN, tax rate, stock, country of origin, seller ID, images, search attributes. |
| Search and browse | Sort, filter, out-of-stock behaviour. No paid ranking without a visible label. |
| Cart and checkout | Single seller vs multi-seller cart, shipping fee rules, coupon rules, minimum order. |
| Orders | States: created, paid, confirmed, packed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled, return requested, return picked, refunded. Who can move each state. |
| Payments | Prepaid success/failure/pending, COD, refund partial and full, idempotency key so a double tap does not double-charge. |
| Seller portal | Onboarding status, KYC pending/approved/rejected, add listing, stock, orders, labels, settlements (read-only to start). |
| Admin | User lookup, order override with reason code, seller suspend, coupon create, CMS banners, role permissions. |
| Reviews | Verified purchase only, report abuse, no editing after a defined window. |
| Notifications | SMS/email/push templates and which events send them. |
| Support | Ticket linked to order ID. |
| Finance | Settlement calculation worksheet and a daily reconciliation export. |

### 11.4 Non-functional requirements

- Languages at launch (English, and whether Hindi or another language is in version 1).
- Currency INR only at launch.
- Performance targets you will actually test (for example, product page response time on a mid-range phone).
- Availability target and maintenance window.
- Audit log on admin actions that change price, refund, KYC, or role.
- Backup once a day minimum, and point-in-time recovery if you use a managed database.

### 11.5 Architecture decision record

Pick and write down:

- Clients: responsive web first, or web + Android together. iOS can follow once the API is stable.
- API style: HTTPS JSON API, versioned (`/v1`).
- Database: relational database for orders and money. Do not put orders only in a document store.
- File storage for images and KYC (separate buckets and IAM).
- Background jobs: payment webhooks, invoice PDF, courier assignment, settlement run.
- Environments: local, staging, production. Payments in test mode on staging.

### 11.6 Data model (minimum entities)

User, Address, Seller, SellerKyc, Category, Product, Variant, Inventory, Cart, CartItem, Order, OrderItem, Payment, Refund, Shipment, Return, Coupon, LedgerEntry, Payout, Ticket, ConsentLog, AdminAuditLog.

Money fields: integer paise, never floating point. Every ledger line is append-only.

### 11.7 API outline

Resource list, auth (bearer session or JWT with short life and refresh), and webhook endpoints for the payment gateway and courier. Error format: stable code, human message, request ID.

### 11.8 Roles and permissions matrix

A spreadsheet is enough: rows are actions (issue refund, approve KYC, edit price, export customers), columns are roles. Two people should be required for large refunds (maker-checker) once volume exists.

### 11.9 Integration list

| System | Data you send | Data you store back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment gateway | Amount, order ID, customer contact | Payment ID, status, method |
| SMS / email | Phone or email, template variables | Message ID, delivery status if available |
| Courier | Pickup and drop address, weight, COD amount | AWB, status, remittance |
| GST / e-invoice (when applicable) | Invoice payload | IRN, acknowledgement |
| Maps / pin codes | Pin code | Serviceable yes/no, city, state |

### 11.10 Design documents

- Sitemap of public site, customer app, seller app, admin.
- User flows: signup, browse, checkout prepaid, checkout COD, cancel, return, seller first listing.
- UI kit: type, colour, buttons, form errors, empty states.
- Content: empty-state text, error text, and notification templates in the languages you launch with.

### 11.11 Test and release documents

- Test plan for checkout, refund, and settlement (these three are not optional).
- Cases: payment success, payment failed after debit, webhook delayed, duplicate webhook, partial refund, COD refused, return after delivery.
- Release checklist: migrations, secrets, DNS, store listing, rollback.
- Staging uses test payment keys only.

## 14. Internal SOPs to write before the first real order

Short pages, owned by a named person:

1. Order confirmation and cancellation.
2. Packing and handover to courier.
3. COD remittance matching.
4. Return quality check and refund.
5. Seller payout run and exception list.
6. Customer complaint handling and escalation times.
7. Counterfeit or prohibited-listing takedown.
8. Payment mismatch (paid in gateway, not marked in app, and the reverse).
9. Production access and who can read customer data.
10. What to do if a government or police request arrives (do not hand over data from a personal phone; route to the grievance/nodal contact and counsel).

## 19. Open decisions that block writing the final policies

Answer these and the lawyer/CA drafts become specific instead of generic:

1. Private Limited confirmed, and in which state is the registered office?
2. Version 1: only BuyyMart-owned inventory, or sellers from day one?
3. Which categories in the first release? (Food and medicines change the licence list.)
4. Cities or pin codes in version 1?
5. Prepaid only, or COD as well?
6. Own warehouse, or only courier pickup from sellers?
7. Company legal name (it can differ from the brand BuyyMart)?
8. Who is the grievance officer (name and email)?

Once those eight answers exist, the next concrete drafts are: terms of use, privacy notice, seller agreement, and the order-state PRD.
